ChatS IM is an open-source SMS, MMS and phone app built around a virtual-number ("role") system: use several numbers for messages and calls on a single SIM, useful for carrier multi-number services.

Messages
• Conversation list grouped by role, with unread badges and pinning
• Chat timeline merging SMS/MMS messages and call history
• Scheduled messages and direct reply from notifications
• User rules: add tags, block, or move to the recycle bin by phone number, role, content, SMS or call record
• Recycle bin with optional automatic cleanup
• Delivery reports
• Import and export of messages and call history

Calls
• Call log with automatic grouping of consecutive calls from the same number
• Incoming call screen and dialer, with call history merged into conversations

Roles (virtual numbers)
• Every role has its own message prefix and call prefix
• Outgoing messages and calls automatically use the matching prefix
• Incoming messages and calls are recognized by their prefix and assigned to the role
• Multiple roles, per-role SIM binding and quick switching

Contacts
• Contact list with account-source filter, groups, pinned contacts and last-contacted time
• Editable contact details and SIM card import

Interface
• Material 3 with dynamic color, light and dark theme
• Bottom tabs on phones, navigation rail on large screens
• English and Simplified Chinese

Privacy
• No ads, no trackers, no network permission; fully open source (GPL-3.0)

Not supported: RCS/5G messages, sending MMS, call recording, caller-location lookup.

Note: to work as your default SMS and phone app, the app needs the corresponding system permissions.
